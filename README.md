##  New Idea Engineering

**New Idea Engineering Sdn. Bhd. (NIE)** is a Malaysia-based engineering and manufacturing company specializing in **customized engineering solutions, precision metal fabrication, and industrial equipment manufacturing**.

We provide **one-stop engineering solutions**, from mechanical design and prototyping to fabrication, welding, machining, and assembly, serving industries such as semiconductor, medical, and industrial manufacturing.

Our mission is to deliver **high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective engineering solutions** that meet our customers' needs.

- ⚙️ Mechanical Design & Engineering
- 🔩 Precision Sheet Metal Fabrication
- 🏗️ CNC Machining & Welding
- 🛠️ Jig & Fixture Manufacturing
- ⚡ Electrical Engineering
- 📦 Product Assembly & Integration
